# Favorites and Notes are private, and there is no social surface

A User's Favorites and Notes are visible only to that User: no public like counts, no other-user comment lists, no activity feed, no sharing or following.
The app is a personal annotation layer over a public corpus, not a community. This is why there is no moderation, no report flow, and no content-visible-to-strangers problem — none of those are missing features, they are absent by construction.

## Consequences

- Do not "fix" this by adding a public favorite count to the character card; that reintroduces moderation, abuse, and privacy problems deliberately kept out.
- A social version is a different model, not a tweak: it needs public authorship, counts, and moderation as new concepts.
